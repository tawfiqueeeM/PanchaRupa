# Staffing, Payroll Approval & Month 24 Financial Close Rules

## 1. Attendance & Shift Meals Policy
- **Meal Allowance Invariant:** Every worker receives exactly one cooked meal per attended factory shift.
  $$\text{MealAllowance} = \text{AttendedShifts} \times \text{BDT } 120.00$$
- Meals are tracked separately from gross taxable wages and charged to Factory Production Overhead rather than direct labor payroll.
- Benchmark: 2 workers × 26 attended shifts × BDT 120 = **BDT 6,240.00 / month**.

## 2. Four-Stage Payroll Governance Workflow
1. `SUPERVISOR_REVIEW`: Shift floor supervisor confirms shift logbook attendance hours and overtime.
2. `HR_VERIFICATION`: Human resources validates leave balances, medical records, and statutory deductions.
3. `FINANCE_RECONCILIATION`: Accounting computes gross earnings, deductions, net bank transfers, and verifies cash availability.
4. `OWNER_AUTHORIZATION`: Founder / Managing Director signs off on bank disbursement batch.

## 3. Month 24 Financial Close & Accrual Profit vs Cash Reconciliation

### Profit vs Realized Bank Cash Bridge
$$\text{RealizedCashFlow} = \text{PBT} + \text{Depreciation} - \Delta\text{AccountsReceivable} - \Delta\text{Inventory}$$
- PBT: +BDT 300,000.00
- Add back non-cash Depreciation: +BDT 10,833.33
- Less Unpaid Broker Receivables: -BDT 280,000.00 (Delayed auction settlements)
- Less Cash Locked in Unsold Stock: -BDT 150,000.00 (Winter finished goods buffer)
- **Realized Operating Cash Position**: **-BDT 119,166.67 (Negative Operating Cash Flow)**
> **Key Management Takeaway:** While the enterprise is profitable on an accrual basis (+BDT 300k), delayed auction collections and inventory buffer investments result in a temporary cash deficit, requiring working capital financing before the next green leaf purchasing cycle.
